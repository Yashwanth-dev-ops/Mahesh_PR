Overall

The motion craft is good: one custom ease-out curve everywhere, presses that scale to 0.97, transform-only animation, and reduced motion respected in every file. The problems are about where the motion is spent. The screens a student opens dozens of times a day replay an entrance on every tab change, while the one-time landing intro makes people wait over four seconds before they can log in. On phones, cards give no feedback when tapped, a third of the small buttons are under 44px, and on a laptop the content column changes width from page to page.

Issues to address
CriticalEmil

Every tab change replays a staggered page entrance

What.ap-main > * fades and slides up 8px over 360ms, with the 2nd and 3rd blocks delayed 40ms and 80ms. It runs on every page in the student app: each tap on Home, Subjects, Papers, Notes, or a chapter's Next button.

Why it mattersMoving between tabs is the most frequent thing a student does, often dozens of times in one revision session. Each change costs about 440ms before the page is fully readable, and the motion is noticed every time. Emil's rule: the more often it happens, the less it should move.

Recommended motionRemove the entrance from .ap-main. If a soft change is wanted, use opacity only, 150ms, with no slide and no stagger. Keep entrances for one-off screens (sign-up done, account status).

The landing hides "Student login" and the main button for over 4 seconds

WhatOn the first visit of each session, the nav (with Student login) fades in at 4350ms and the "Sign in with roll number" button at 4250ms. Until then they are invisible but still in the page.

Why it mattersA student who opens the link to log in before an exam has to wait 4.4 seconds for the button. The scene itself is a good one-time moment; blocking the only action on the page is the problem. Each new session (a new tab, the next day) plays it again.

Recommended motionTake .lp-nav out of the intro so it shows from the first frame, and bring the button in right after the disc forms (about 1200ms). Let the stickers, phone and headline letters play on behind them.

On a laptop, the content column changes width on every page

What.ap-main has max-width: 880px; margin: 0 auto inside a grid cell, so it shrinks to fit its content. Measured left edge at 1440px: Home 630px, Subjects 664, Papers 644, Notes 576, Profile 566, Chapter 483, Search 436. Home ends up as a narrow 412px strip with empty space on both sides, and its subject grid collapses to one column.

Why it mattersClicking from Home to Search shifts every heading about 200px sideways. It reads as a layout bug and breaks the student's sense of place.

Recommended motionNo animation needed. Add width: 100% to .ap-main so every page shares the same 880px column. Only the sidebar highlight should move.

ImportantJakub

Spacing doesn't follow one scale, and the phone edges don't line up

WhatOn phones the top bar's logo sits 20px from the edge while all content sits at 24px, so the logo is 4px off the content line on every student screen. Across the CSS there are 26 different spacing values; 3, 6, 10, 14, 18, 22 and 26px fall off a 4px grid (form gap 22px, landing gaps 18/22/26px, list gaps 10px next to grid gaps 12px). Page side margins are 24px for students and 16px for admin on the same phone.

Why it mattersEach value is close but not the same, so nothing quite lines up. On a 390px screen a 4px misalignment between the logo and the heading below it is visible.

Recommended motionChange .ap-top padding to 0 12px 0 24px. Then move to one scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64 (for example 22→24, 10→12, 14→16, 18→16, 26→24). Gaps in lists and grids: 12px everywhere.

ImportantJakubEmil

Cards and bottom tabs give no feedback when tapped on a phone

WhatCards (subjects, chapters, results, Previous/Next) only react to a mouse hover. The bottom tab links have no :active state. Only the yellow pill buttons turn off the grey tap flash (-webkit-tap-highlight-color), so Android shows a blue/grey box on everything else.

Why it mattersPages render on the server, so after a tap there can be 200–600ms before anything changes. With no press feedback, students tap again, which then opens the wrong chapter or double-toggles.

Recommended motiona.ap-card:active { scale: 0.98 } and .ap-link:active { background: var(--surface) }, both at 120ms with --ease-out. Set -webkit-tap-highlight-color: transparent once on html.

ImportantJheyEmil

The landing loops never stop and can't be paused

WhatThe ring and tags orbit forever (48s), the PDF tag floats forever (3.2s), and the phone demo replays every 14s for as long as the page is open. Reduced-motion users are covered, but nobody else can stop it.

Why it mattersAccessibility rules (WCAG 2.2.2) ask that anything moving for more than 5 seconds can be paused. Endless motion next to the headline also pulls the eye while people read the copy.

Recommended motionPlay the phone demo twice (animation-iteration-count: 2) and let it rest on its final frame, which is already designed as the "after" state. Pause the orbit on hover/focus of the scene. Pausing when the tab is hidden comes free with the browser.
